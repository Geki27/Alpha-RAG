4. Construction
The reinforcement panel shall be constructed from Zylon (PBO) fibres, impregnated with a
toughened epoxy resin system. The nominal cloth weight should be 300gsm. A compliant material
would be ACG MTM49-3 ZF0100 (295gsm 2x2 Twill Zylon HM). The panel may include a single
external ply of carbon fibre, impregnated with a toughened epoxy resin system, not exceeding
300gsm. If different resins are used for the carbon and Zylon plies, they must be co-curable.
Carbon fibre, to the same specification, may also be used for any part of the reinforcement panel
outside of the minimum area of coverage defined in Figure 1.
The construction of the panel shall be quasi isotropic filling a closed mould tool and shall avoid
darts, joins or gaps in any ply, apart from those required to cover cut-outs for ventilation holes as
shown in Figure 1. The panel shall be essentially void free.
The panel must be cured to the manufacturer’s recommended cure cycle.
4. Attachment to Visor
The panel will be bonded to the visor over the entire surface area with an adhesive film or paste.
The choice of adhesive may allow for the panel to be removed for servicing.
